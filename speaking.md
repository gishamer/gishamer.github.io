# Speaking

I speak about the decisions that turn AI into an organizational capability: architecture, domain knowledge, evaluation, and technical governance.

## Topics

- **Operating AI agents across teams:** shared architecture, identity, tool access, and oversight.
- **Making domain knowledge reusable:** agent skills, expert ownership, and the practices needed to maintain them.
- **Evaluating AI in real workflows:** baselines, release decisions, and the relationship between evaluation and guardrails.

## Short bio

Flurin Gishamer is AI Czar at Open Systems, where he defines AI platform architecture and priorities and guides adoption across teams. His work connects reusable domain knowledge, evaluation, and technical safeguards. He writes Undisconnected and teaches workshops on designing and operating AI agents.

## Extended bio

Flurin Gishamer is AI Czar at Open Systems. He introduced the company’s agent mesh approach and reusable agent skills, defines the AI platform architecture and roadmap, and coordinates implementation. He sets the technical direction for evaluation and guardrails and leads the Data & AI Guild.

His background includes developing foundational RAG, evaluation, and guardrail capabilities for production AI. He coauthored a SwissText 2024 paper on reliability in customer operations and has spoken at SwissNLP and CH Open Workshop-Tage. In his publication Undisconnected, he examines AI architecture, domain knowledge, adoption, and the evidence needed to assess whether AI improves the work.
